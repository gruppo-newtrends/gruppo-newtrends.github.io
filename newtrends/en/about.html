In the centre of Lugano since 1991

New Trends SA was founded in 1991 as a real estate fiduciary, after the owner obtained his fiduciary licence. At first the work was mainly brokerage. Over time administration, development, design, technical work and marketing were added.

In about ten years we built and sold around 80 homes, houses and flats. Today the total is over a hundred. We design with the market in mind: every choice has to support the property's value.
