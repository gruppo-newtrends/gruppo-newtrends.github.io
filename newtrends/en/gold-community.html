The Community in short

The Community Gold New Trends is the private community of people we know in person. Many properties reach our members first, and only then the online portals.

There is one way in: the Qualifica Gold, a personal consultation of about 30 minutes at our office in Via Pretorio 11, Lugano, by appointment and at no cost or obligation. We discuss area, budget, financing and timing, and record your search.

From then on you are a member: when a suitable property comes in, we present it to you before it is published online. You receive the Gold member card, by hand at the office or by post, and access to the members' private group.

For owners: your property is presented first to qualified buyers, discreetly.

It is not a discount, it gives no special prices and it does not guarantee that every property goes through the Community first. You join only through the Qualifica Gold at our office: there is no online sign-up.
