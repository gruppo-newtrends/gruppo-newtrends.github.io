How it works

  1. 1

    Your answers

    Type, area, size, condition and features of your property.

  2. 2

    The indicative estimate

    A price or rent range based on our data, with the calculation shown.

  3. 3

    The visit

    Our agent for your area calls you and, if you wish, visits the property to set the final value.

What the estimate is based on

On the prices of the homes we have for sale or to let, and those we have sold or let, in Ticino (101 properties, updated 14.11.2026). We use only average values by type, region and municipality, with at least three different projects behind each value, adjusted for year, size, condition, outdoor space, view, energy efficiency and parking. Today we have enough data mainly for the Luganese and Mendrisiotto areas; for other areas our agent will give you a first indication in person.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing: the online valuation and the first visit are at no cost and with no obligation.

Is the estimate a formal appraisal?

No. It is an indicative figure calculated automatically. It does not replace a formal appraisal and should not be used with banks, authorities or in an estate. We set the final value after the visit.

Who will contact me?

Our agent responsible for the area where the property is, usually by the next working day. You will not receive automatic WhatsApp messages.

What happens to my data?

We use it only for this request and to assist you as an owner. You can ask to see it or have it deleted at any time at info@new-trends.ch.
