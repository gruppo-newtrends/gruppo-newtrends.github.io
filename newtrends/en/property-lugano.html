Lugano

Lugano is Ticino's largest city and Switzerland's third financial centre. Most of our work happens here: 85% of the sales and lettings we closed in the last two years were in the Lugano district. We know each district, from the pedestrian centre to Viganello, Porza and Cadro.

Good to know

  • Train to Zurich: about 2 hours
  • Train to Milan: about 1h15
  • USI university and LAC arts centre
  • Towns and districts: Lugano, Viganello, Porza, Cadro, Paradiso, Massagno

Frequently asked questions

Which parts of Lugano do you cover?
