Mendrisiotto

17 homes · from CHF 274,000

Available homes

Mendrisiotto is the southern tip of Ticino, around Mendrisio, Chiasso and the Merlot vineyards. It is close to Como and Milan and linked to Lugano by TILO trains. Here we offer affordable flats and income buildings with good yields.

Good to know

  • TILO trains to Lugano: about 20 minutes
  • Academy of Architecture in Mendrisio
  • Monte San Giorgio, UNESCO World Heritage
  • Towns and districts: Mendrisio, Rancate, Ligornetto, Chiasso, Stabio, Balerna
