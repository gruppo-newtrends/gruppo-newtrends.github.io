Collina d'Oro and South Lugano

18 homes · from CHF 344,000

Available homes

Collina d'Oro is the hill south-west of Lugano, with Gentilino, Montagnola and Agra. It is a quiet, sunny residential area a few minutes from the centre. TASIS international school is here, and Hermann Hesse lived here. We also cover South Lugano, including Barbengo.

Good to know

  • Lugano centre: about 10 minutes by car
  • TASIS international school in Montagnola
  • Hermann Hesse Museum
  • Towns and districts: Gentilino, Montagnola, Agra, Barbengo, Carabbia
