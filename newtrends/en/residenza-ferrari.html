The project

Living in nature, close to the city

Residenza Ferrari is a small building of just eight homes in the centre of Tesserete: three residential floors plus a garage, facing south-west, around a large old beech tree that the project chose to keep.

Every home has its own outdoor space, a garden or a terrace, and is handed over turnkey with high-end finishes. The interiors can still be adapted to your needs.

Finishes and technology

Quality you feel every day

Designed to use little energy and last a long time: efficiency, acoustic comfort and carefully chosen materials.

  • AIL Solar photovoltaics

    A high-yield solar installation.

  • Air-to-water heat pump

    Latest generation, with underfloor heating.

  • Advanced insulation

    High-performance thermal and acoustic insulation, professional waterproofing.

  • Triple glazing

    Soundproof, with motorised aluminium shutters.

  • Ready for the future

    Pre-wired for home automation and electric car charging.

  • Security

    Video intercom and modern security systems.

  • Turnkey

    High-end finishes, customisable interiors.

  • Private laundry

    Washing machine and dryer for each home.

The location

Tesserete, the heart of Capriasca

Tesserete is the main village of the municipality of Capriasca: schools, shops, banks, a sports centre and restaurants, in quiet green surroundings a few kilometres from Lugano.
